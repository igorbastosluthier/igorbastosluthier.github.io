Handmade flutes from the Vale do Capão

I am Igor Bastos, a flute maker and fife player. In my workshop in the Vale do Capão I make bamboo and wooden pífanos, Irish flutes and Native American style flutes, each one made to order and tuned hole by hole across three octaves.

What I make

Prices in Brazilian reais (BRL), before options and shipping.

Bamboo pífano

Six-hole transverse flute from the fife bands of Northeast Brazil, in one piece of bamboo (Bambusa tuldoides).

from R$ 260

Wooden pífano

One piece, inlaid (marchetaria) or in 2 parts, with a conical head bore made with reamers Igor makes himself.

from R$ 690

Irish flute, Low D

Simple-system wooden flute: cylindrical head, conical body, six holes, no keys. 2 or 4 parts.

from R$ 2.100

Native American style flute

Bamboo or wood, single, double (with drone) or with a carved wooden totem. Pentatonic minor scale.

from R$ 380

Sound

What shapes the sound most is the bore and a well-finished interior. Each wood has its colour and grain, but the difference in sound between one wood and another is subtle. Woods I work with today: jacarandá, muirapiranga, roxinho, maçaranduba, araribá and sebastião-de-arruda.

How to order

  1. Message me on WhatsApp with the model, key and wood you want, or what the flute is for.
  2. I reply with a full quote.
  3. Making time, counted from the deposit: wood in 1 or 2 parts, 2 months; in 3 or 4 parts, 3 months; bamboo, about 1 month.
  4. During the making, I send you photos and videos of your flute. Every flute comes with a fingering chart.

International shipping

I ship from Palmeiras, Bahia. International shipping is available on request: send me your country and postal code and I will check the options and the cost.
